Script generationAuto-generate the narration script for a chapter. Claude Code reads the chapter's panels through your local Claude login. Tone and style below are per-project.
Batch automationRunning the pipeline for several chapters at once. Claude work is held back when your Claude subscription usage reaches these limits.
When on, each chapter waits after its frames are exported until you have reviewed it in the editor and clicked Continue. When the 5-hour limit below is reached, Claude work waits for the window to reset and then resumes by itself; chapters already past Claude keep going. The weekly limit always asks you first. A Windows notification when a chapter finishes or fails, the pipeline finishes, or a usage limit holds it. When free space on this PC drops below the limit, each finished chapter is backed up to the project's Drive folder and its frames, audio, page images and video are cleared from this PC (they come back when needed). Only works for projects stored in a Drive folder; a chapter is cleared only once it is verified in Drive.
Video sizeHow much each rendered chapter weighs. Smaller files use a lower resolution and a blurred background; the moving background video is what costs the most.
Frame layout
Applies to the next render of each chapter. Merging keeps the chapters' size (no re-encode) when they all match.
Background musicPlays under the narration for the whole video.
VolumeNarration loudness and the music bed level. Applies to the next render of each chapter (Re-render voice is not needed).
Narration voiceChatterbox clones a voice sample (samples are converted to 24kHz mono automatically). Kokoro is faster but cannot clone: pick one of its voices.
NOTEChanging the voice doesn't touch your frame selection. Use Re-render voice in the chapter editor to regenerate narration only.

Export projectSave this whole project as one .zip - chapter downloads, boxed frames, scripts, audio, renders, uploaded media and settings - to back it up, reinstall the app, or move it to another PC. Restore it with "Import project" on the Projects screen.
